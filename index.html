About / elevation

I spent three years turning briefs into experiences — luxury weddings, social events, and the moodboards, branding and spatial systems behind them.

What I learned designing rooms turns out to be exactly what digital design asks for: understand the person you are designing for, build a system that holds together, and guide them through it with intention. I am now bringing that thinking to branding, web and UX — and this site is the first piece of evidence.

Design System / legend

The legend for this sheet.

Every choice on this site is a decision, not a default. The same tokens run across every page — and the theme toggle in the nav proves it: only the token block changes, never a component.

01 — PaletteTwo tones
Plaster#E9E9E6
Ink#17181A
Crimson#B11E2F
Petrol#134E48

Crimson carries the event & wedding heritage — it is the markup pen, used on roughly five percent of any view so it always reads as emphasis. Petrol is the cool, digital counterweight: the pivot, made visible.

02 — TypeThree roles
DisplayBodoni Moda
BodySchibsted Grotesk
MonoIBM Plex Mono

A couture Didone for voice, a clean grotesque for clarity, a technical mono for the annotations in the margins. Big jumps between sizes do the work of hierarchy.

03 — Space8px rhythm
Base unit8 px
Scale×1 ×2 ×3 ×4 ×6 ×9
Max width1200 px
Corner radius0 — drafted, not soft

Hairline guide-lines and right-angled corners borrow from a drawing sheet — the same plan-view logic an event needs and a screen needs.

04 — MotionRestraint
Revealfade + 22px rise
Easingcubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
Hoverlift · draw · magnet
Reduced motionfully respected

Motion confirms an action or rewards attention — never decorates. Turn on “reduce motion” and the site stays perfectly usable.
