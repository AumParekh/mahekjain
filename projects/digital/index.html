The portfolio you are reading is itself the case study — a design system rendered as a living, themeable site. The pivot into digital, demonstrated rather than claimed.

You’re looking at it
Role
Design + build
Year
2026
Scope
Design system · site
Stack
HTML · CSS · JS
Challenge

Prove a pivot without a digital portfolio.

Moving from event design into UX/web means the strongest evidence isn’t a deck — it’s a working product. The site had to argue, in its own structure, that the systems thinking from spatial design transfers cleanly to screens.

Approach

A drafting sheet, built from tokens.

One stylesheet defines every colour, type role, space step and motion curve. Two themes — Plaster and Blueprint — share that token block, so the light/dark toggle literally re-skins the whole site without touching a single component. The navigation states the information architecture out loud; motion confirms actions and never decorates; reduced-motion and keyboard users are first-class.

Look at the nav: theme toggle, scroll-progress dimension line, and a structure numbered 01–06 — the IA, on display.

Outcome

A site that is its own proof.

Fast, static, accessible, and themeable — hosted with no build step. The single-source-of-truth claim isn’t written in a slide; it’s demonstrable in two clicks of the toggle.
